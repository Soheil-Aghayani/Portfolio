## 🔬 Core Research & Master's Thesis

<div align="center">
  <table>
    <tr>
      <td width="100%" align="center">
        <h3>🌱 Biofuel Production from Waste Cooking Oil via Transesterification</h3>
        <p><b>Synthesizing Heterogeneous Catalysts from Waste Seashells for Circular Economy Impact</b></p>
        <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/TRL_Status-Level_4_(Lab_Validated)-1A7F37?style=flat-square&labelColor=333333" />
        <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Supervisors-Dr._R._Samie_Zafraghandi_%7C_Dr._M._Baghdadi-0969DA?style=flat-square&labelColor=333333" />
      </td>
    </tr>
  </table>

> **Abstract & Highlights:** This ongoing research focuses on the valorization of marine biomaterials (oyster/seashell waste) into high-performance calcined heterogeneous catalysts. By coupling **experimental lab trials** with **Python data pipelines** and **SimaPro Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)**, this project establishes a scalable, low-emissions pathway to convert discarded edible oils into clean biodiesel.
